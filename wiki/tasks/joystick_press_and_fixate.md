# joystick_press_and_fixate

This is the second step of the [joystick family](family-joystick.md). It combines the
joystick hold with fixation. The settings file is a near copy of `joystickPress_settings.m`
in which state 3 is renamed to `showFix` and the hold-duration variables are replaced by
fixation variables.

## Trial flow

- `waitForJoy`: a press strobes `joyPress` and goes to `showFix`. If there is no press
  within `joyWaitDur`, the trial ends as `nonStart`.
- `showFix`: the fixation point is drawn in white, cyan or green, chosen at random once
  per trial. `fixOn` is timed with [postFlip](../concepts/drawing-and-postflip.md). If gaze
  enters the window while the joystick is held, within `fixWaitDur` of `fixOn`, the run
  strobes `fixAq` and goes to `dontMove`. A release gives `joyBreak`. A timeout strobes
  `nonStart`.
- `dontMove`: once `fixDurReq` has passed since `fixAq` with gaze still in the window, the
  run strobes `hit`, logs `timing.fixHoldReqMet` and plays the high tone. Leaving the
  window gives `fixBreak`. A release gives `joyBreak`.
- `hit`: reward is delivered `rewardDelay` s after `fixHoldReqMet`. The trial ends
  `postRewardDuration` + `p.rig.dp.dacPadDur` + reward duration later.

## Conditions

There is no trials array. `nextParams` draws `fixDurReq` uniformly from
[`fixDurReqMin`, `fixDurReqMax`] (0.2–0.5 s). It jitters the fixation point by
`(rand − 0.5)·fixLocRandX/Y` (15 × 10 deg) around `fixDegX/Y`, and sets the point's size
from `fixPointRadPix` / `fixPointLinePix`.

## Key trVars

The GUI shows `rewardDurationMs` (200), `rewardDelay` (0.1 s), `fixDurReqMin/Max`,
`fixWinWidthDeg/HeightDeg` (6 × 6 deg), `fixDegX/Y`, `fixPointLinePix`, `fixPointRadPix`
and `passJoy` / `passEye`. `fixWaitDur` is 5 s.

## Data & strobes

- Timing: `joyPress`, `fixOn`, `fixAq`, `fixHoldReqMet`, `fixBreak`, `joyRelease`,
  `reward`. `p.trVars.fixDegX/Y` holds the jittered location used on the trial.
- strobeList: date and time only (built with `fliplr`).

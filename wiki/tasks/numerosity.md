# numerosity

numerosity extends [seansFirstTask](seansFirstTask.md)'s one-vs-two discrimination to
electrical stimulation. On **visual** trials (`trialType` 1) the subject sees one or two
stimuli, as in seansFirstTask. On **microstimulation** trials (`trialType` 2) the "stimulus" is
a pulse train delivered through one or two electrodes via Ripple (`pds.xippmex 'stimseq'`). In
both cases the subject saccades to target 1 for one stimulus and target 2 for two. The directory
is `tasks/numerosity (WiP)/`, and commit 9c0aa4cc describes the task as a copy of seansFirstTask
being edited into spatial and temporal versions with stimulation trials added.

## Trial flow

`trialBegun` → `showFix` (the `waitForJoy` state is skipped and `joyBreak` is commented out) →
`dontMove` (stimulus delivered, targets appear, fixation goes off at `timeFixOffset`) →
`makeSaccade` → `checkLanding` → `holdTarg` → `sacComplete`, which branches to
`correctTarget` (21, reward) or `wrongTarget` (22). Not leaving fixation within `goLatencyMax`
ends in `heldFix` (23). Microstimulation is triggered inside the run loop: Ripple's
`fastsettle` is applied to the recording channels, `microStimOn` is strobed, and the
`stimseq` command is sent for one or both electrodes. The fixation period has a fixed total
length (`totalFixDur`: 0.9 s spatial, 1.3 s temporal), and `nextParams` places the stimulus and
target events inside it.

## Conditions / trial structure

`initTrialStructure` has one table per experiment type, with columns `trialType`, `numStim`,
`stimDur` (ms for visual trials, number of pulses for microstim), `interStimInterval`,
`stimPeriod`, `currentThresholdMultiplier`, `interElectrodeSpacing`, repetitions and
`trialCode`:

- **spatial** (`numerosity_settings_spatial.m`): visual 1- or 2-stimulus trials (120/167/200 ms);
  single-electrode microstim at 1.5/2.0/2.5× threshold; two-electrode microstim with
  inter-electrode spacing in 75 µm steps (in the current table only the 375 µm rows have
  non-zero repetitions). Trial codes 24111/24112/24121/24122.
- **temporal** (`numerosity_settings_temporal.m`): visual single stimuli of 240–790 ms and pairs
  separated by 30–390 ms; single-train microstim controls matched in pulse count, duration or
  frequency; and two trains separated by 30–390 ms. Trial codes 24211/24212/24221/24222.

For microstim trials, `nextParams` picks a random pair of channels from
`p.init.electrodeInfo.goodElectrodes` at the required spacing (`electrodeInfo.mat`, loaded in
the settings file) and checks that the mapped Ripple channels can stimulate. Amplitude is the
electrode's `C50` × `currentThresholdMultiplier`, capped at 200 µA and applied in steps of 2 µA.
If Ripple is not recording and `stopIfNotRecording` is true, `nextParams` stops with an error.

Besides the experiment type, the two settings files differ in fixation and onset timing, the GUI
variable list, and fixation-point color (red for spatial, green for temporal).

## Data & strobes

`p.init.taskCode` is set in the settings file from `uniqueTaskCode_numerosity` (32017). In
`numerosity_settings_spatial.m` the strobeList sends `taskCode`, the row's `trialCode`, `trialEndState`, and the channel and
amplitude of each electrode (`microStimChannel` / `microStimCurrAmp` sent twice per trial, once
per electrode, in electrode order). Microstim delivery times are saved as
`timing.microstimOneSent` / `microstimTwoSent`.

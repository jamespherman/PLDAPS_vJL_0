# joystick_release_for_fix_dim

This is the first task in the family with a signal-detection outcome structure. On
**change** trials, the fixation point dims after a random hold, and the subject must
release the joystick within a latency window. On **no-change** trials, the subject must
keep holding until the trial ends. This step replaces fixation *offset* in
[joystick_release_for_fix_off](joystick_release_for_fix_off.md) with graded *dimming*, so
detection difficulty can be set by the dim level.

## Trial flow

`waitForJoy` → `showFix` → `dontMove` work as in the earlier steps. When `fixDurReq` is
met with gaze in the window:

- On a change trial, the run logs `timing.fixHoldReqMet` (the dim time), redraws the
  fixation point in CLUT slot `expFixDim_subFixDim` and goes to `makeDecision`. A release
  before `joyMinLatency` strobes `fa`. A release between `joyMinLatency` and
  `joyMaxLatency` strobes `hit` and stores `timing.reactionTime = time − fixHoldReqMet`.
  No release by `joyMaxLatency` gives `miss`.
- On a no-change trial with the joystick still held, the run strobes `cr` and plays the
  high tone.
- A release during `dontMove`, before the dim, strobes `fa`. Leaving the fixation window
  gives `fixBreak`.

Feedback: `hit` gives the high tone and a reward after `rewardDelay`, `cr` gives the high
tone, `miss` gives the low tone, and `fa` gives the noise tone.

## Conditions

There is no trials array. `nextParams` → `trialTypeInfo` handles the trial type:

- `isChangeTrial = rand < propChangeTrials` (default 0.8).
- On change trials, `p.trData.dimVal` is chosen with equal probability from
  `lowDimVal` / `midDimVal` / `highDimVal` (0.75 / 0.825 / 0.9). On no-change trials it is
  0. The dimmed colour is `bg + dimVal·(white − bg)`. It is written into the
  `expFixDim_subFixDim` CLUT row and pushed with `Datapixx('SetVideoClut')` before the
  trial (see [colour and CLUT](../concepts/color-and-calibration.md)). A larger `dimVal`
  means a brighter point after dimming, which is a smaller and harder change.

`locationInfo` jitters the fixation point by `fixLocRandX/Y` (24 × 16 deg). `timingInfo`
draws `fixDurReq`.

## Settings variants

- `joystick_release_for_fix_dim_settings.m` is the main file. The GUI shows
  `low/mid/highDimVal`, `propChangeTrials`, `joyMinLatency` / `joyMaxLatency` (0.2 / 1 s),
  `fixDurReqMin/Max` and `rewardDelay`.
- `joystick_release_for_fix_dim_settings_DEBUG.m` keeps the fix-off variables
  (`propRelTrials`, `maxJoyRelLatency`), sets `passEye = 1`, and uses the fix-off CLUT
  index list.

## Data & strobes

- `p.trData.dimVal` holds the post-dim brightness; 0 marks a no-change trial.
- `p.trData.timing`: `fixHoldReqMet` (dim time, or hold end on no-change trials),
  `joyRelease`, `reactionTime`, `fixAq`, `fixOn`, `reward`.
- `updateOnlinePlots` appends to `p.status.trialEndStates`, `reactionTimes` and `dimVals`.
  It plots outcome against time from fixation acquisition, and computes performance over
  the last `numTrialsForPerfCalc` trials.
- strobeList: date and time only. Outcome codes `hit`, `cr`, `fa`, `fixBreak` are strobed
  from the state machine.

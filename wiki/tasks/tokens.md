# tokens

A Pavlovian reward task. After a variable ITI, an image cue appears at the screen center.
If the monkey holds fixation on it for `fixDur`, a row of 1–9 "tokens" (dots) appears
above fixation. After `outcomeDelay`, the tokens are "cashed in" one at a time: each one
delivers a juice pulse of `rewardDurationMs`, with `juicePause` between pulses. Each cue
predicts the token count from either a **normal-like** or a **uniform** distribution
(both span 1–9 with mean 5), so reward uncertainty is manipulated while the mean stays
fixed. Uncued trials skip the cue and the fixation requirement. The `tokens_AV` variant
adds a flickering-token, frequency-sweep-tone "audiovisual" outcome with a probability set
per cue (0, 0.5 or 1). The analysis plan is in
[tokens_analysis_planning.md](../../analysisPlanningDocs/tokens_analysis_planning.md), and
`actions/analyzePupilPE.m` analyzes pupil reward- and sensory-prediction-error responses.

## Trial flow

States are in the generated block. `trialBegun` sets `ITI_EndTime` from `p.trVars.iti`.
`waitForITI` then goes to `showCue` when `isFixationRequired` is true, or straight to
`showOutcome` on uncued trials. `showCue` puts the cue on (postFlip `cueOn`) →
`waitForFix` (gaze in window before `cueOn + fixAqDur`, otherwise `nonStart`) → `holdFix`
(`fixDur`; leaving the window gives `fixBreak` and a low tone) → `showOutcome` (postFlip
`outcomeOn`; on AV trials the DataPixx audio sweep schedule starts) → `cashInTokens` (starts
at `outcomeDelay`; one `pds.deliverReward` per token; the drawn tokens disappear as they
are cashed in) → `success`. In AV trials the tokens cycle through 8 DKL hues
(`p.draw.flickerColors` from `initClut`), `flickerFramesPerColor` frames per hue.

## Conditions / trial structure

`initTrialStructure` builds a compact `p.init.trialsTable` (one row per condition ×
reward amount, with an `nReps` column) and expands it into a cell-array
`p.init.trialsArray`. Reward amounts are fixed in advance rather than drawn at run time:

- **Normal-like** (`dist = 1`): frequencies `[1 1 2 3 4 3 2 1 1]` for 1–9 tokens (18 trials per cue).
- **Uniform** (`dist = 2`): 2 trials at each of 1–9 (18 per cue).
- **Fixed** (`dist = 0`, uncued `blank.jpg`): 18 trials at 5.

| exptType | Conditions | Block |
|---|---|---|
| `tokens_main` (`tokens_settings.m`) | 3 normal cues (`famNorm_01/02`, `novNorm_01`), 3 uniform cues (`famUni_01/02`, `novUni_01`), uncued with tokens, uncued without tokens (`isToken = false`); trialCodes 27001–27008 | 144 trials |
| `tokens_AV` (`tokens_AV_settings.m`) | 6 normal and 6 uniform cues (familiar and novel × `avProbability` 0 / 0.5 / 1), plus the 2 uncued conditions; trialCodes 28001–28014 | 252 trials |

`nextParams/chooseRow` samples the available rows without replacement. An aborted trial
(end state 11–19) is repeated on the very next trial (`p.status.repeatLast`). When the block
is used up, `updateTrialsList` rebuilds the array from `trialsTable`. On every trial the ITI is
drawn from an exponential with mean `itiMean`, truncated to [`itiMin`, `itiMax`]. For
`tokens_AV`, `isAVTrial` is drawn from the row's `avProbability`. Token positions are
computed in `nextParams` from `tokenBaseX`, `tokenBaseY` and `tokenSpacing`. Cue images are
loaded in `generateStimuli` from a `stimuli/tokens` folder (the repo's `stimuli/tokens/`
holds the `fam*`/`nov*` images and `blank.jpg`).

## Key trVars and settings variants

Defaults: `fixDur` 0.5 s, `fixAqDur` 10 s, fixation window 6°, `rewardDurationMs` 65,
`juicePause` 0.5 s, `outcomeDelay` 1 s, ITI mean/min/max 3/1/5 s, `passJoy` 1.
`tokens_AV_settings.m` differs from `tokens_settings.m` in `exptType`, in defining
`tokenBaseX`/`tokenBaseY`/`tokenSpacing` as trVars (also shown in the GUI, in place of
`fixWin`, `fixDegX` and `fixDegY`) and `flickerFramesPerColor` (6), and in removing the static `p.stim.token.pos`. The rig config is chosen
from the hostname (`rigConfig_rig<N>`).

## Data & strobes

The end-of-trial strobeList (wrapped in `fliplr`) strobes:

- session date and time;
- `REWARD_AMOUNT_BASE` with value `REWARD_AMOUNT_BASE + rewardAmt`;
- `rwdAmt`;
- `taskCode` = `uniqueTaskCode_tokens`;
- `trialCount`;
- `trialCode`.

In-trial strobes are `trialBegin`, `CUE_ON` (via postFlip), `fixAq`, `nonStart`, `fixBreak`,
`OUTCOME_DIST_BASE + dist` at outcome onset, one `reward` per token (from
`pds.deliverReward`), and `trialEnd`. Saved per trial: the trVars (`dist`, `cueFile`,
`isFixationRequired`, `isToken`, `rewardAmt`, `iti`, and `isAVTrial` for AV), eye
position and pupil (`eyeP`), and timing fields including `cueOn`, `fixAq`, `fixBreak`,
`outcomeOn` and `reward`. Field-by-field reference:
[tokens_data_dictionary.md](../../data_dictionaries/tokens_data_dictionary.md).

## Session practice & decisions

The `initTrialStructure` header records why reward amounts are pre-specified: so that the
normal and uniform distributions cover the same integer range (1–9) with the same mean (5),
and so that every condition occurs equally often in a block.

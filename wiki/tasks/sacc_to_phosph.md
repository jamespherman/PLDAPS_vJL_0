# sacc_to_phosph

The directory is `tasks/sacc_to_phosph (WiP)/`. A phosphene-detection task: the subject
fixates, and after a random delay one of three things happens. A small visual stimulus
may appear somewhere on the screen, Ripple may deliver an intracortical microstimulation
train, or nothing may happen. If
something was presented, the subject should saccade away from fixation. If nothing was,
the subject should keep fixating. Rewards go to saccades on stimulus trials (hits) and to
held fixation on no-stimulus trials (correct rejects). The session measures detection as
a function of current amplitude and stimulation parameters, with visual trials as a
behavioral anchor. The RF-mapping tasks [rfMap](rfMap.md) and [barsweep](barsweep.md)
export per-channel RF-center CSVs that their action headers describe as consumed manually
by this task.

## Trial flow

`trialBegun → showFix → dontMove → makeSaccade`, then one of two branches (see the
generated state list):

- **Saccade:** the eye leaves the fixation window between `goLatencyMin` and
  `goLatencyMax` (0.05–0.3 s) after `stimOn`, then `checkLanding` (lands in the target
  window within `maxSacDurationToAccept`) → `holdTarg` (`targHoldDuration` = 0.15 s) →
  `sacComplete`.
- **Hold:** fixation is kept in `makeSaccade` until the time since trial start
  (`timeNow`) exceeds `timeFixOffset = totalFixDur` (1.5 s) → `heldFix`.

`sacComplete` is rewarded for trial types 1, 2, 4–7 (stimulus present), and `heldFix` is
rewarded for type 3 (no stimulus). The reverse outcomes end without reward. Leaving
fixation before `goLatencyMin` → `fixBreak`. Leaving after `goLatencyMax` →
`lateSaccade`. Leaving the target early → `failedToHoldTarg`. No fixation → `nonStart`.
These four aborts set `trialRepeatFlag`, and `updateTrialsList` returns the row to the
pool. `waitForJoy` is defined but not used by the run loop; `passJoy = 1` and `_finish`
calls `pds.waitForJoystickRelease`.

The stimulus window opens at `timeStimOnset` (uniform 0.3–1.3 s after fixAq) and lasts
`stimDur` (0.10–0.20 s). On visual trials a texture is drawn and `stimOn`/`stimOff` are
strobed. A screen grab is saved as `p.trData.stimScreenshot` on the flip after onset. On
microstim trials `_run` applies Ripple fast-settle to the recording channels, strobes
`microStimOn` and sends the stimulation sequence with `pds.xippmex('stimseq', …)`.

## Conditions / trial structure

`initTrialStructure.m` builds a table with columns `trialType, stimAmplitude, cmdPeriod,
cmdRepeats, cmdSeqLength, cmdSeqIPI, no of trials, trialCode`. It repeats each row
`no of trials` times. `nextParams.m/chooseRow` picks a random remaining row.
`trialCode` is 24000 + trialType. Trial types: 1 visual, 2 one-channel microstim,
3 no stimulus, 4 two-channel opposite polarity, 5 N-channel same polarity,
6 microstim on a `stimList` set, 7 microstim with parameters from the table,
8 biomimetic (in the pick_one_channel table with 0 repetitions; not handled by the
trial-type switch in `nextParams.m`).

| Settings file (`p.init.exptType`) | Table rows (reps) | Amplitude rule | Electrode choice (GUI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_pick_all_channels` (primary) | visual 1, no-stim 1, type 6 0 | staircase over `ampVals` (22 values, 1–210) | `stimListIndex` into `stimList` (sets of `[electrodes; polarities]`) |
| `_pick_one_channel` | visual 15, no-stim 50, type 5 35 (types 2, 4, 8 at 0) | staircase over `ampVals = [1 3 5 8 12 14 18 23 28]` | `stimulatedElectrode`, `numStimElectrodes` |
| `_random_sample` | visual 2, no-stim 6, 15 type-7 rows × 1 | method of constant stimuli: amplitude ∈ {1, 12, 24, 36, 48} × `cmdSeqLength` ∈ {3, 4, 5}, `cmdPeriod = 100`, `cmdRepeats = 20`, `cmdSeqIPI = 2` | `stimulatedElectrode`, `numStimElectrodes` |

Staircase (the two staircase variants): two hits on microstim trials step to the next
lower amplitude, and two misses step up. `overrideStaircase > 0` fixes the amplitude.
Changing the electrode or set resets the staircase. In `random_sample`, `ampVals` and the
per-electrode hit/miss tables are built from the trial table at init. Before
stimulating, a confirmation dialog appears if the total current exceeds 420 (amplitude ×
number of electrodes) or more than 4 electrodes are selected (`safetyCheckFlag`).

## Key trVars and settings

Stimulation pulse parameters come from the GUI in the two staircase variants
(`cmdPeriod`, in 33.3 µs clock cycles = 30000/frequency; `cmdRepeats`; `cmdSeqLength`;
`cmdSeqIPI`) and from the table in `random_sample`. Visual stimuli are a random oval or
rectangle, 0.1–0.5 dva and randomly rotated, placed uniformly within x ∈ [−24, 24],
y ∈ [−14, 14] dva. The target window scales with eccentricity
(`visTargWinWidthDeg ^ (1 + d/36)`). On microstim and no-stim trials the target window is
30 × 20 dva centered on the screen, so any saccade counts. `electrodeInfo.mat` holds
`rippleChannel` (1 × 64 map from electrode number to Ripple channel) and
`goodElectrodes`. In pick_one_channel and random_sample, a warning is printed once when
the chosen electrode is not on that list. If `electrodeInfo` has `predictedRFX/Y`, pick_one_channel draws a predicted-RF
circle on the experimenter display. Differences between the three settings files are
otherwise limited to the GUI variable lists, `rewardDurationMs` (120 or 200), the
fixation window (1.5 or 1 dva) and the default `cmdPeriod`/`cmdRepeats` (100/50 in
pick_all_channels, 150/10 in pick_one_channel). `p.init.sessionId` ends in the `exptType`.

## Data & strobes

Per-trial `trData` (via `pds.saveP`) includes the timing fields
(`fixOn`, `fixAq`, `stimOn`/`stimOff`, `saccadeOnset`/`Offset`, `targetAq`,
`microstimSent`, `reward`, `trialEnd`), `stimScreenshot` on visual trials, and `strobed`.
`p.trVars.stim` stores a copy of `p.stim` each trial. The strobeList sends `taskCode`
(`uniqueTaskCode_sacc_to_phosph` = 32019), `trialCode`, `trialEndState`, and one
(code, value) pair per stimulated electrode for `microStimChannel`, `microStimCurrAmp` and
`microStimPolarity` (`pds.strobeTrialData` expands vector values). The generated block
above is parsed from the primary settings file. Live counters (visual hits/misses,
per-electrode × amplitude microstim hits/misses, correct rejects, false alarms) feed an
online psychometric plot (`updateOnlinePlots`). There is no data dictionary.

# gSac

gSac is the stock guided-saccade task and the root of the
[gSac family](family-gsac.md). Its settings header describes it as follows.
The fixation dot appears and the subject acquires fixation. A target then
appears elsewhere and either stays on (visually guided) or disappears (memory
guided). After a delay the fixation point disappears, which tells the subject
to saccade to the target, and the subject is rewarded. It dates from the
initial commit (2022-08-04) and records with Plexon Omniplex. Later commits
were cross-task maintenance (mouse eye simulation in 166581bf, `strobeNow` for
behavioural events in 61d7a932).

## Trial flow

The states are the shared gSac set (see the generated list). In
`gSac_run.m`, saccade onset is any exit from the fixation window between
`goLatencyMin` and `goLatencyMax` (0.1–0.5 s) after the go signal, with no
velocity criterion. Landing must reach the target window within
`maxSacDurationToAccept` (0.1 s) of onset. On memory trials the target is
re-illuminated `targetReillumDelay` (0.15 s) after landing. Event times are
`timeNow` values in the state and timing machines.

## Conditions / settings variants

- **`gSac_settings.m`** (primary): `targLocationPreset = 'preset_quatro'` defines two RF points, (10, 5) and (−6, −4), each with a partner rotated 90°, in `p.stim.xy{1..4}`. It also sets `setTargLocViaTrialArray = true`. `initTrialStructure.m → gSac4_v1` builds a trial array of 4 locations × vis/mem, with 4 reps at locations 1 and 3 and 2 reps at locations 2 and 4. The trial codes are 20100–20401. `chooseRow` picks any remaining row at random, and errored rows stay available (`updateTrialsList`). Other timing: `targetFlashDuration` 0.2 s, onset U(0.4, 0.7) s, go at onset + U(1, 2) s, and `rewardDurationMs` 150.
- **`gSac_settings_trialsArray.m`**: an older variant with its own rig fields and a `rigConfig_rigB_20180712.m` rig file, `exptType = 'all_locs'`, `passEye = 1`, and a shorter strobeList without taskCode/date fields.

Mouse- and GUI-placement flags and the `grid`/`ring`/`nRing` presets also
exist (`initTargetLocationList`).

## Data & strobes

`gSac_finish.m` strobes the strobeList and `trialEnd`, stops Omniplex
(`pds.stopOmniPlex`), and pulls Omniplex data when `p.rig.connectToOmniplex`
is set. It then calls `pds.saveP` and updates the trial list. No saccade
metrics are computed. The strobeList (generated table) includes `connectPLX`,
`passJoy`, `joyPressVoltDir` and `trialCode`, and location is strobed as
`targetTheta`/`targetRadius`. There is no data dictionary for this task.
